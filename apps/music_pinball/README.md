# Music Pinball

A pocket pinball table that turns collisions into music. Tap the playfield to launch, then hold its left or right half to raise that flipper. Two fingers can control both flippers on supported screens.

Each game has three balls. Hit notes within 2.5 seconds to build a multiplier (up to ×5); follow the glowing bumpers to finish a melody for 1,000 points. Complete three melodies to start a 15-second double-score fever with richer sounds. Wrong notes keep your melody progress; losing a ball ends fever. A ball lost within four seconds of launch gets one automatic save. Tap to retry after game over. Best score lasts until the app exits.

Tap the pause symbol at the top to pause/resume, or the back arrow at the bottom to return to the Launcher. Audio runs at 70% and restores the previous device volume on exit.

Requires a touch display of at least 240×240; scales to 360×360 vocat and 480×480 Mosaico. Sound requires firmware with `audio.sample_player`; the game remains playable silently if audio is unavailable. Browser preview is not enabled.

All three short PCM samples and the icon are original procedural assets. The Chinese UI uses bundled Noto Sans CJK font subsets; see `assets/FONT-LICENSE.txt`. No external music or app dependencies are required.
